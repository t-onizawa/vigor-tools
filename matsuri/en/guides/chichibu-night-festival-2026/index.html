Chichibu Night Festival 2026 — Practical Visitor Brief

Chichibu, Saitama · Dec 2 (Wed) and Dec 3 (Thu), 2026

Information last checked: Nov 13, 2026 (Japan time). Every item below links to the Japanese official page it comes from.

The 2026 festival has ended. This page is kept as an archive of the 2026 information and is no longer updated. Check the official sites for next year's dates.

The Chichibu Night Festival (Chichibu Yomatsuri) is the annual festival of Chichibu Shrine, held on Dec 2 and Dec 3 every year. Six huge floats are pulled through the town; on the night of Dec 3 they are hauled up the steep Dango-zaka slope while fireworks are fired nearby. This brief covers the practical side: when, what is closed, where to watch, how to get home, and what to carry.

At a glance

  • OfficialDates: Wed Dec 2 (Yomiya, the eve festival) and Thu Dec 3 (Taisai, the main festival). The climax is the night of Dec 3.
  • OfficialDec 3: the town centre around Chichibu Shrine is closed to vehicles 10:00–22:00, and parking or stopping is banned across the whole city.
  • OfficialAround the Otabisho (Chichibu Park) and Dango-zaka, entry is restricted from about 17:30 to 22:30.
  • OfficialFireworks: Dec 2, 19:00–20:00 (about 500 shots); Dec 3, 19:30–21:55 (about 4,000 shots).
  • OfficialChichibu Railway runs no trains between Chichibu and Kagemori stations from about 19:30 to 22:30 on Dec 3.
  • OfficialThe official ticketed float-viewing seats for Dec 3 are sold out.
  • Not announcedThe 2026 float procession timetable (including when the floats climb Dango-zaka) had not been published as of Nov 11.

Dates & hours

OfficialSix floats take part: two kasaboko (Nakachika and Shitago) and four yatai (Miyaji, Kamimachi, Nakamachi and Motomachi). The Chichibu Tourism Association describes Dec 2 as closer to a pre-festival evening, with four floats pulled around town, and Dec 3 as the main day with all six.

Temporary car parks

On Dec 3 there are eight temporary car parks, all marked on the official map. A fee is charged even if you only drop someone off. Fees per vehicle: car (including kei cars) ¥1,000 at P1–P8; motorbike ¥500 at P1–P8; medium-size vehicle ¥3,000 and large vehicle ¥5,000 at P1 only. On Dec 2 only P3 is open, 9:00–21:00, and it is free.

Floats and the Dango-zaka climb

OfficialFloats weigh up to about 20 tonnes. The climax is the night of Dec 3, when they are pulled up Dango-zaka, a steep slope beside Ohanabatake Station. On the 2026 map, the floats start from Chichibu Shrine at about 18:00 and travel to the Otabisho (Chichibu Park) by way of the main street and Seijin-dori.

Fireworks

  • OfficialDec 2 (Wed): 19:00–20:00, single shots and starmines, about 500 shots.
  • OfficialDec 3 (Thu): 19:30–21:55, about 4,000 shots. The map lists three parts from 19:30 and a finale at about 21:50. Times may change.
  • OfficialBoth nights are fired from Hitsujiyama Park, which is closed to visitors. The city names two good places to watch: the Chichibu City Hall car park (the official fireworks viewing area) and the square in front of Seibu-Chichibu Station.

Checked Nov 13, 2026. Official source (Japanese): Chichibu City information page · 2026 route and traffic map (PDF).

Seat tickets for float viewing

  • OfficialThe Chichibu Tourism Association sold ticketed "B seats" for float viewing on Dec 3: ¥4,000 per person, all seats reserved, no seat requests.
  • OfficialThe official sales were in Japanese only, through two routes: in person at the Yao Travel Salon (Yao Department Store, 5th floor, Chichibu; cash only), or by postcard application. Both sales periods have ended.

Trains and getting home

Seibu Railway (Ikebukuro to Seibu-Chichibu)

  • OfficialOn Dec 3 Seibu adds limited express trains from Ikebukuro to Seibu-Chichibu from the 11:00 hour, about every 30 minutes. The printed timetable lists limited expresses leaving Ikebukuro from 11:30 to 17:30, and the last one listed arrives at Seibu-Chichibu at 18:54.
  • OfficialFor the trip home, Seibu adds up limited express trains and extra local trains between Hanno and Seibu-Chichibu. On Dec 3, limited expresses for Ikebukuro leave Seibu-Chichibu at 18:24, 18:46, 19:24, 19:54, 20:24, 20:54, 21:24, 21:54 and 22:24 (the 22:24 arrives at Ikebukuro at 23:44).
  • OfficialThe last departure on the printed timetable is a local train from Seibu-Chichibu at 23:14 for Tokorozawa (arriving 0:27). A limited express leaving at 23:04 goes only as far as Tokorozawa (arriving 0:07).
  • OfficialOn Dec 2, one up limited express was changed to start at Seibu-Chichibu instead of Hanno. The announcement does not give the time.
  • OfficialLimited express tickets for Dec 3 went on sale on Nov 3. Seibu has not published whether seats remain. Check the Seibu site.

Chichibu Railway (Kumagaya to Chichibu)

  • OfficialThe overhead wire at the Dango-zaka crossing is disconnected while floats pass. From about 19:30 to 22:30 no trains run between Chichibu and Kagemori stations. A free shuttle bus runs between Kagemori Ground and the town, so you can board at Kagemori Station.
  • OfficialA special steam locomotive train, the "SL Chichibu Hikiyama Yomatsuri-go", runs on Dec 3 from Gyoda toward Chichibu only (Gyoda 15:23, Kumagaya 15:37/15:43, Chichibu 17:55). All seats are reserved, with no sales on board.
  • OfficialChichibu Railway asks passengers with a transport IC card to top it up in advance: if the balance is too low the station gate will not open. It also advises buying your return ticket early because ticket counters get crowded.
  • Not announcedThe notice does not state the last train times for Dec 3. Check the Chichibu Railway timetable.

How to confirm your way home

  1. Open the Seibu Dec 3 festival timetable (PDF) and note the last train you can realistically catch.
  2. Check Chichibu Railway's Dec 3 notice for the 19:30–22:30 gap between Chichibu and Kagemori.
  3. Settle on your return train before the fireworks begin at 19:30. Our note Stations will be crowded after the fireworks end at 21:55.

Avoiding the worst crowds

Official sources do not publish crowd forecasts. These suggestions rest only on the official facts above.

  • Our noteConsider Dec 2. The Tourism Association describes it as closer to a pre-festival evening: four floats, the street closure ends at 20:00, and the fireworks run 19:00–20:00. The Dango-zaka climb is the Dec 3 highlight, so Dec 2 is a quieter way to see the floats, not an equal substitute.
  • Our noteTake the train, not the car. The city itself asks visitors to use public transport, and the whole city has a parking and stopping ban on Dec 3.
  • Our noteArrive in the afternoon. Restrictions and viewing points begin at 17:00–18:30, and the pavement viewing point is open only from 19:00 to 21:30.
  • Our noteDecide your way home before 19:30, because the Chichibu Railway gap and the Seibu timetable both depend on when you leave.
